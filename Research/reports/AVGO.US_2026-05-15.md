## 1. Company Overview

Broadcom (NASDAQ: AVGO) is a diversified semiconductor and infrastructure software company. Market cap ~$575B at prior close ($439.79). Revenue ~$55B TTM. Key segments: Semiconductors (~60%: networking chips, custom AI ASICs, wireless), Infrastructure Software (~40%: VMware integration, CA Technologies, Symantec).

## 2. Financials

- Revenue growth: ~+25% YoY TTM (VMware adds ~$20B incremental)
- Gross margin: ~75–78% (industry-best, driven by software mix)
- P/E (forward): ~33x — premium but supported by software gross margins
- VMware acquisition ($61B, 2023): Integration proceeding; converting perpetual licenses to subscription
- AI networking revenue: Custom AI ASICs (XPUs) for Google, Meta; Ethernet networking for hyperscalers

## 3. Competitive Position

- **AI ASICs**: Broadcom is Google's primary custom silicon partner (TPU variants). Meta and others exploring custom AI chips through Broadcom.
- **AI networking**: Tomahawk/Trident switches dominate cloud networking; AI Ethernet is growing market.
- **Software**: VMware's hybrid cloud platform is a near-monopoly in enterprise virtualization.
- **Moat**: VMware installed base creates extraordinary switching costs ($1B+ migration cost for large enterprises).

## 4. Catalysts

- **AI ASIC revenue**: XPU partnerships with hyperscalers — Broadcom guided $60–90B in AI ASIC TAM through 2027. Even 5% market share = $3–4.5B incremental.
- **VMware subscription conversion**: Every license converted to subscription increases revenue predictability and margin.
- **Next earnings**: FY Q2 2026 (fiscal year ends October) — scheduled approximately early June 2026. Near-term earnings risk present.
