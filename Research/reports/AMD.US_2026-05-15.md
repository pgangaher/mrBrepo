## 1. Company Overview

AMD (NASDAQ: AMD) is the #2 semiconductor company globally by revenue and the only credible challenger to NVIDIA in high-performance GPUs. Market cap ~$285B at open ($440.60). Revenue ~$25B TTM. Key products: Ryzen CPUs, EPYC server CPUs, Instinct MI300X AI GPUs, Radeon GPUs.

## 2. Financials

- Revenue growth: ~+20% YoY TTM; data center growing much faster
- Gross margin: ~50% (improving as data center mix increases)
- P/E (forward): ~35–40x — premium to historical but justified by AI GPU ramp
- Debt: Modest; strong cash generation improving
- Data center revenue: MI300X AI GPU ramping — $4–5B run rate, targeting $7B+ in 2026

## 3. Competitive Position

- **CPU**: EPYC holds ~25% of server CPU market and gaining share from Intel; strong moat in HPC
- **AI GPU**: MI300X is the only credible alternative to NVIDIA H100/H200 for AI inference. Microsoft Azure, Meta, Oracle have publicly committed to MI300X deployments.
- **Software gap**: ROCm ecosystem is ~18 months behind CUDA — the primary obstacle to displacing NVIDIA in AI training workloads
- **Key advantage**: Open ecosystem positioning — hyperscalers want an alternative to NVIDIA monopoly pricing

## 4. Catalysts

- **MI300X expansion**: Every % gain in AI GPU market share translates to $1B+ incremental revenue
- **EPYC Turin**: Next-gen server CPU with performance advantages over Intel Granite Rapids
- **Data center capex cycle**: $300B+ annual hyperscaler capex in 2026 drives GPU/CPU demand
- **Next earnings**: Q2 FY2026 — expected late July 2026 (no near-term earnings risk)

## 5. Risks

- **Extreme momentum**: +118% in 3 months, +74% in 1 month. Mean-reversion risk is high at these valuations.
- **RSI 76.70**: Approaching overbought — RSI often peaks between 75–85 in AMD's momentum cycles before 20–30% corrections.
- **CUDA moat**: AMD's ROCm ecosystem remains inferior; most AI training still runs on NVIDIA hardware.
- **Concentration risk**: Revenue increasingly concentrated in data center — any AI capex slowdown would hit AMD disproportionately.
- **Stop width issue**: ATR $22.90 creates a mechanical stop 5.73% below entry — exceeds Phase 1 rule.
