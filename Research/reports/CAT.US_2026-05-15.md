## 3. Competitive Position

- **Moat**: Brand + dealer network (>3,700 dealers globally) + aftermarket parts revenues — switching costs are very high in heavy equipment
- **Mining exposure**: CAT equipment critical for lithium, copper, cobalt mining — direct beneficiary of EV/clean energy mineral extraction
- **Construction**: US infrastructure spending (IIJA) driving demand; international infrastructure in Middle East, Southeast Asia strong
- **Key competitors**: Komatsu (Japan), Deere (overlapping in construction), Volvo CE, Liebherr
- **Services segment**: Aftermarket parts + services growing to ~25% of revenues — adds recurring, higher-margin cash flows

---

## 4. Catalysts

1. **US infrastructure**: IIJA ($1.2T) and CHIPs Act construction driving multi-year demand
2. **Mining capex**: Critical mineral extraction requires CAT equipment; copper and lithium mining CapEx up
3. **Data center construction**: Hyperscaler AI facilities require massive earthmoving and construction — CAT primary beneficiary
4. **International markets**: Middle East giga-projects (NEOM, etc.) and Southeast Asian infrastructure build
5. **Services growth**: Autonomous mining solutions (Cat Command) growing double-digit

---

## 5. Risks

1. **Cyclicality**: Heavy equipment is highly cyclical — a global recession is the primary risk
2. **China exposure**: ~5% revenue from China; slowdown already impacting some construction segments
3. **Inventory normalization**: Dealer inventories elevated in some regions — could cap near-term orders
4. **Valuation multiple**: P/E ~36–40x is high for a cyclical — any earnings miss or macro deterioration triggers de-rating
5. **Labor/steel costs**: Input cost inflation, though recent moderating trend is positive
