## 3. Competitive Position

- **Novelis moat**: Novelis is the global leader in flat-rolled aluminium and recycled aluminium — serves automotive, packaging, consumer markets across Americas, Europe, Asia
- **EV tailwind**: Auto sector's shift to aluminium body panels (lightweight) benefits Novelis directly
- **Copper synergy**: Birla Copper adds diversification and India domestic exposure
- **Key competitors**: Nalco (domestic peer), Vedanta Aluminium, globally: Arconic, Constellium, Aleris
- **India domestic**: Hindalco controls ~30% of Indian primary aluminium capacity

---

## 4. Catalysts

1. **EV adoption**: Every EV uses 3–4x more aluminium than an ICE vehicle — Novelis is the direct beneficiary
2. **Packaging demand**: Can and foil packaging growing in emerging markets — Novelis's fastest growing segment
3. **Copper cycle**: Energy transition requires massive copper buildout (EVs, charging infrastructure, grid upgrades)
4. **Aluminium prices**: LME aluminium recovering; India domestic demand growing
5. **India infrastructure**: Railways, aerospace, and defence using more aluminium — domestic demand structural

---

## 5. Risks

1. **Commodity price risk**: Both aluminium and copper are cyclical — global slowdown would hurt
2. **Novelis leverage**: High debt at Novelis level constrains financial flexibility
3. **Energy cost sensitivity**: Aluminium smelting is energy-intensive; power cost inflation is a risk
4. **China competition**: Chinese overcapacity in aluminium keeps global prices suppressed
5. **Currency risk**: Novelis revenues in USD/EUR; consolidated results affected by INR/USD moves
